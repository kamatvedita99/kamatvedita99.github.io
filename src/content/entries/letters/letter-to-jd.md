Hi Jd,

How are you doing? I miss you deeply. We all do. The team feels different without you. There's a quiet sadness that lingers. I can only imagine how much you must miss work too. You were always so connected with everyone, always speaking to people, work was your world. I know this must be hard for you, in ways none of us can fully understand.

Life rarely goes the way we expect. One moment, we assume everything will stay on track, and the next, it all shifts. Unexpected turns leave us stunned. I don't know what exactly you're going through, but I do know this: it must be heavy.

But Jd, the way you cope, with silence, with complete withdrawal..it scares me. I know it's your way, and I won't judge that. But bottling it all up, carrying it alone... that's not healing. That's hurting. You've been through so much. Losing your mother just last year, and still showing up for everyone. I saw that strength, and I admired it. But I also worried, and still do.

Maybe it's silly, but I've always seen myself like a daughter figure in your life. I care about you that way. And now, the only sign I have of your existence is your last seen on WhatsApp. That's how I know you're still here. I've asked people, reached out, but no answer has really settled my heart.

Part of me wants to scream at you for cutting me off like this. But the bigger part just aches to do something to help and can't.

I wonder, do you even care that I care this much? Am I being foolish? Am I overdoing it? Maybe. But I can't not care. And I don't think you're in the right place right now. I just hope you're fighting through it.

You've always had a wide circle, but do you have someone you can actually fall apart in front of? I hope you do. Please don't slip into depression again. Please don't keep everything in. Talk to someone. Anyone. You journal, I know, but I hope you speak too.

Maybe this is a time to pause and rethink everything. You've worked so hard. Maybe now is the moment to dream again. To build something. Mentor someone. Write. Travel. Heal. You have the time, and I hope you give yourself that permission.

Maybe that's easier for you, or maybe I just don't matter as much. I try not to take it personally. but how can I not feel something?

Still, no matter how mad I get, my care is always greater. You're someone special to me, like a mentor, a father figure, a godfather rolled into one. So no, I can't stay mad at you. I can't stop caring either. And I sure as hell can't stop wondering how you're doing. I feel stuck. Left behind. But it's okay.

I feel stranded, yes. But I also feel love - pure, stubborn, unconditional love for someone who meant the world to me. Maybe you'll never understand how deeply I care. Maybe you don't have to. I just hope you're safe, and healing, and that life brings good things your way again.

I won't trouble your quiet anymore. No more messages. No more emails. But just know wherever you are, I'm thinking of you, rooting for you, and carrying you in my prayers.

With love,

Vedita
